## Retained lessons

- Justify a new table by its question, estimand, aggregation, source and added
  information; prefer adapting an existing display over adding redundancy.
- Keep ranks, gaps and fitness effects distinct, and avoid repetitive historical
  methodological explanations in the scientific narrative.
- Distinguish actual seeds from synthetic metadata and independent samples from
  justified paired designs.
- Track precision, artifact provenance and issue states; do not infer that
  software was redesigned merely because the final analysis was reconciled.

The archive move did not change article sources, figures, numerical results,
datasets, the supplementary package, or computational methods.
